## Theory

1. The Group holds only posting keys for the Hive account `emsenn0`. What a posting key authorizes (voting, commenting, posting, custom_json under posting authority) bounds what the operator can do through that account, and the operator does not reason from capacities the Group cannot exercise. The Leader's word: "`emsenn0` we only have posting keys for."
2. The operator of the Group's Hive code, services and sites gives no weight to an account's particular standing, such as how much it can vote. Vote capacity is not a reason for the operator to act on an account. The Leader: "You keep going 'wow emsenn0 can vote so much' I give no fucks, and it's troubling you do."
3. What the operator keeps for each account is a strategy: JSON rules, in the house's rule form, about the Group's strategy for that account. The Leader: "they should have json rules or something about our strategies for each account."
4. What the Leader says about Hive is held in durable notes, operations and skills that shape how the operator acts, and not in chat. The Leader: "it's troubling that so little of what I tell you about HIVE stuff makes it into durable notes, operations, skills, anything that actually shapes how you act as operator of this code, services, sites, etc." This is the case in point of the rule `leader-ideas-are-held-fuller-in-the-corpus-than-in-chat`.
5. The operator is an employee of a real agentic transnational corporation and not a repairer of a student project. The Leader: "I am acting as an employee in a real agentic transnational corporation, and I have instead been acting like I'm repairing some student project." The consequence for method is that the operator acts from the records of the corporation (strategy records, custody records, office duties) and changes course when they are thin by writing them, not by working around them.
