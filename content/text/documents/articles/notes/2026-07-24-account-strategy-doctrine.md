# Account Strategy Doctrine

This note summarizes the fleet accounts' playing strategies so sessions can apply them consistently. Strategies here are CAPABILITIES the accounts must be able to play, not standing orders to trade. The ideas pool records the underlying strategy concepts. Execution always
passes the evidence gates in
content/text/documents/designs/2026-07-23-agentic-self-improvement-wiring.md.

## The strategy classes

- **stack-token**: each account may have one token that is its "stack sats" —
  the thing it accretes as its primary long position. Meeting-criteria tokens
  acquired under any other strategy are KEPT, never flipped.
- **income staking / tokens that print**: hold or stake tokens whose holding
  mints more tokens (Hive-Engine mining tokens, distribution tokens).
- **community-curation staking**: stack tribe/Scot tokens that scale curation
  rewards in specific communities — and let what we hold BIAS the curation
  strategy (curate where our stake curates powerfully).
- **tip tokens**: hold above tip-bot thresholds so stake converts to daily
  distributable tips — another curation layer.
- **governance accrual**: WORKERBEE is admitted as "part of how we get stuff
  for doing governance on hive-engine, the sidechain" — stacking it is a
  governance position, not just yield.

## Per-account approach

- **michael561 — the accretor.** Prefers not-trading, just accreting —
  expressly a different philosophy than sigmund-vexler. Its stack-token is
  **WORKERBEE**. Its liquid HIVE and HBD are to become printing/curation/tip
  tokens per the strategy classes above; what it acquires it keeps. Its
  curation should tilt toward communities where its stake curates powerfully.
- **sigmund-vexler — the trader.** The alpha-hunter archetype (existing
  aagaard.config.json record; AGRODAO sigmund-hive-arbitrage-enterprise
  charter): bounded arbitrage proposals, positions are entered to be exited.
- **help-fund — the patron-steward.** Existing charter (AGRODAO
  help-hive-liquidity-enterprise): deploys settled surplus into liquidity,
  monitored by the exit-value lane.
- Other accounts (emsenn0, essiebee, marshian) keep their aagaard.config.json
  archetypes; emsenn0 additionally authors the fleet's public methodology
  posts (e.g. "Your Hive Engine balance is not your exit value", 2026-07-22).

## What follows for builders

A lane capability for michael561 must support one-way accretion chains
(HIVE/HBD → SWAP.HIVE → token → stake) with typed refusal on anything that
would SELL a strategy-qualifying holding; sell-side machinery belongs to
sigmund-vexler's lanes, not michael561's. The general capital-deployment-yield
assay die (work order wo-finance-and-market-services-section-capital-deployment-yield-52256bb45725)
prices lanes; this doctrine picks which priced lanes each account may play.
